A bond ladder for USDC on Solana

Five maturities. One deposit. Leave on any day.

Deposit USDC and the vault splits it across five rated debt instruments that mature in 3, 6, 9, 12 and 18 months. You see the whole ladder before you sign, and the price to leave before you leave.

Open the app

Runs on Solana devnet with demo USDC, a demo catalogue of fictional issuers and our own ratings. Not audited.

01

The strategy treasurers use, without the brokerage account

A bond ladder splits money across bonds that mature at different times, so part of it comes due regularly instead of all at once. In traditional finance it takes a brokerage account, minimum lots and manual reinvestment. Here it takes a wallet.

02

How it works

  1. 1 — Pick a profile.

    Conservative takes nothing rated below A-, Balanced nothing below BBB-, and each caps how much one issuer can hold. Nothing below BBB- is ever bought.

  2. 2 — See the ladder before you sign.

    Each rung's instrument, rating, maturity and amount is on screen first. Whatever cannot buy a whole unit stays in your wallet.

  3. 3 — Deposit once.

    The whole ladder opens in one transaction.

  4. 4 — Leave on any day.

    Exit all of it or part of it before maturity. USDC comes back in the same transaction, from a backstop pool the protocol funds — not from other depositors.

03

The less time left, the less it costs to leave

The exit pays the position's current value, minus a 0.5% yearly management fee owed so far, minus a spread that grows with how long the pool will have to hold your instruments. A ladder close to maturity leaves almost for free.

You see all four figures before you sign, and the transaction carries a floor: it cannot pay you less than the quote by more than 0.1%. If the pool is short, the exit is refused whole and you are offered the largest share it can pay.

What this isn't (yet)

Not yet

  • The ladder does not roll yet: when a rung matures, nothing reinvests it, so after the first maturity it stops being a ladder.
  • Nothing reacts to a rating downgrade yet.
  • The maintenance keeper is not running.

Not in this demo

  • Ratings are not Moody's or AlphaLedger. They come from our own oracle program over a fictional catalogue of issuers.
  • The instruments are not real bonds. Our own program issues and redeems them at an oracle price.
  • There is no slippage or market depth: the route is deterministic, so a quote matching its payout proves the arithmetic, not behaviour on a real market.
  • Devnet only, demo USDC, not audited, no KYC or any regulatory compliance.

Questions

Is this real money?
No. It runs on Solana devnet with a demo USDC token the deployer mints. Nothing here is worth anything off devnet.
Where do the ratings come from?
From our own rating-oracle program, over a catalogue of fictional issuers. The real feed this is designed for — Moody's ratings in token metadata on Solana — is a closed institutional API, so the demo does not use it.
What happens when a rung matures?
Today, nothing: rolling a matured rung into a new 18-month one is the next milestone. Until then the ladder is a ladder only until its first maturity.
